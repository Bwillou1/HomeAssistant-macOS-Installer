System Requirements
==================

- macOS 12.0 (Monterey) or later
- Intel x86_64 or Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3) processor
- At least 2GB of free disk space
- 4GB RAM recommended

Privacy Policy
==============

Home Assistant Desktop processes all data locally on your device. 
No data is sent to external servers except for:
- Optional cloud integrations configured by the user
- Automatic updates (if enabled)

For more information, visit: https://www.home-assistant.io/privacy/
